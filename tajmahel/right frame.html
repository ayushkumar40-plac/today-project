About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world, built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Constructed in 1632, it is made of white marble and is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

It attracts millions of tourists every year due to its architecture, symmetry, and historical importance.
